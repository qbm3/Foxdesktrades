Trading financial instruments involves significant risk and may not be suitable for every investor. Before opening an account or entering into any transaction, you should carefully consider your financial circumstances, investment objectives, level of experience, and risk tolerance. You should only trade with money you can afford to lose and seek independent financial advice if necessary.
