Important Risk Disclosure

Trading financial instruments involves significant risk and may not be suitable for every investor. Forex, cryptocurrencies, indices, and commodities can experience substantial price fluctuations, and you may lose some or all of your invested capital. Trading with leverage or on margin can amplify both profits and losses.

Before opening an account or entering into any transaction, you should carefully consider your financial circumstances, investment objectives, level of experience, and risk tolerance. You should only trade with money you can afford to lose and seek independent financial advice if necessary.
