Why ETFs

A simpler way to diversify

ETFs bundle many assets into one, so you can build a broad portfolio without buying each piece separately.

Spread your risk

One ETF can hold hundreds of companies, so a single trade gives you a slice of a whole market.

Follow an index

Track benchmarks like the S&P 500 or Nasdaq 100 instead of picking individual stocks.

Trade like a share

ETFs are bought and sold on the exchange during market hours, just like a stock.

Types of ETFs

Find the fund for your goal

Whether you want the whole market or one corner of it, there’s an ETF built for it.

Index ETFs

Follow a whole market, such as the S&P 500, the Nasdaq 100 or the Dow.

Sector ETFs

Focus on one part of the economy, like technology, energy or health care.

Commodity ETFs

Get exposure to gold, silver and other raw materials without holding them.

Bond ETFs

Hold a basket of government or corporate bonds in a single position.
