Why bonds

Why add bonds to your portfolio

Bonds can bring income and balance to a portfolio built mainly on shares.

Regular income

Most bonds pay interest at set intervals, giving your portfolio a more predictable stream of returns.

Balance your portfolio

Bonds often move differently from stocks, which can help steady your portfolio when shares swing.

Follow interest rates

Bond prices and yields react to interest-rate news, so you can trade on your view of where rates are heading.

Types of bonds

Know what you’re trading

Bonds differ by who issues them and how much risk they carry. Here are the main kinds.

Government bonds

Issued by national governments, such as US Treasuries and German Bunds.

Corporate bonds

Issued by companies to raise money, usually paying more interest than government debt.

High-yield bonds

Higher interest from issuers with lower credit ratings, with more risk to match.

Bond ETFs

A basket of bonds in one fund that trades on the exchange like a share.
