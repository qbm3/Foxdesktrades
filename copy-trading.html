FAQ

How much will copy trading cost me?

There’s no fee to start copying a trader. The usual costs of each trade, such as spreads, still apply to copied trades, and you can review them in your account before you allocate funds.

How does copy trading work? How do I set up for copy trading?

Pick an expert trader, click copy and choose how much to allocate. From then on, the trades they open and close are mirrored in your account in proportion to the amount you allocated. You can pause or stop copying at any time.

What’s the minimum amount required to copy a trader?

The minimum allocation can vary by trader and account type. It is shown on each trader’s profile before you confirm.

How long does it take for copied trades to be executed in my account?

Copied trades are placed automatically as soon as the trader you follow opens or closes a position, so they run in your account in real time.

Can other traders copy me?

Yes. Experienced traders can apply to become an expert on Civa Trades. Contact support to learn about the requirements.
