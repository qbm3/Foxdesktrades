Account & verification

How do I open an account?

Choose Sign up, enter your details and confirm your email address. You can then log in, verify your identity and fund your wallet.

How do I verify my account?

After you sign up, go to your profile and follow the verification steps. You’ll be asked for a photo ID and proof of address. We’ll email you if we need anything else.

Why do I need to verify my identity?

Identity checks protect your account and help prevent fraud. Financial services providers are generally required to confirm who their clients are before they can trade or withdraw.

How do I update my personal details?

Log in and open your profile to update your details. Some changes, such as your name, may need new documents before they take effect.

I forgot my password. How do I get back into my account?

On the login page, choose “Forgot password” and enter the email address on your account. We’ll send you a link to set a new password. If the email doesn’t arrive, check your spam folder or contact us.

Deposits & withdrawals

How do I deposit funds?

Log in, open your wallet and choose Deposit. Pick a payment method, enter the amount and follow the on-screen steps. Your balance updates once the payment is confirmed.

Which payment methods can I use?

The payment methods available to you are shown in your wallet when you choose Deposit. They can vary depending on your country of residence.

How long do withdrawals take?

It depends on the payment method you use. You’ll see the expected time when you request a withdrawal, and you can follow its progress in your wallet.

Why is my withdrawal pending?

Withdrawals can stay pending while a payment is being processed or if we need to complete a verification check. If it has been pending longer than expected, contact us and we’ll look into it.

Trading & platform

What can I trade on Civa Trades?

You can trade stocks, ETFs, bonds, metals, futures and IPOs. Availability can depend on your country and account type.

How do I place a trade?

Find the market you want, open its chart and choose whether to buy or sell. Enter your amount, review the details and confirm the order.

Can I follow live prices?

Yes. Every market shows live prices and charts, and our live trading page explains the tools available to you.

Do I need trading experience?

No. You can start small, follow experienced traders with copy trading, and learn as you go. Remember that all trading involves risk.

Copy trading

How does copy trading work?

You choose an expert trader to follow and decide how much to allocate. Their trades are then copied into your account in proportion to the amount you set.

How do I choose an expert to copy?

Compare each expert’s track record, trading style and risk level before you start. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Can I stop copying at any time?

Yes. You can pause or stop copying an expert from your account whenever you want.

Security

How do I keep my account secure?

Use a strong password you don’t use anywhere else, turn on two-step sign-in and never share your login details.

Will Civa Trades ever ask for my password?

No. Civa Trades staff will never ask for your password or sign-in codes. If someone does, don’t share them and contact us straight away.

What should I do if I spot a scam?

Don’t click any links or send money. Email us at contact@civatrade.com with the details and we’ll help you check whether it’s genuine.

Investment plans

How do investment plans work?

Each plan has its own minimum deposit and features. You can compare them side by side on our investment plans page.

How do I choose a plan?

Think about how much you want to invest and how involved you want to be. If you’re unsure, talk to an advisor before you decide.

Can I track my plan?

Yes. Log in to see your plan, its current value and your activity at any time.
